Our Mission

The EWB-USA CWRU Chapter has a vision of a more community focused education, both locally and globally, for the students of Case Western Reserve University. These hands-on projects emphasize applying knowledge from the classroom as well as student-led innovative methods.

Chulu Community Charging Station

The Chulu Community is located 2.5km from the park fence and lacks electricity. We are working on designing a charging station to serve this community of over 5500 people.
