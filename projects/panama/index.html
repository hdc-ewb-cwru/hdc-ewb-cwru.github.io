Panama Water System Implementation

[ Photo Spot: Panama Community Site ]

Project Overview

Our team partners with rural partner communities in Panama to assess, design, and construct sustainable, gravity-fed water delivery infrastructure. This project ensures consistent year-round access to clean, potable water.

Key Focus Areas

  • Water quality testing and source catchment design.
  • Gravity-fed pipeline distribution and storage tank construction.
  • Forming local water committees for ongoing system maintenance.
