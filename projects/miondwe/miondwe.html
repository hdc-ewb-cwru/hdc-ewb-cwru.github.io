Miondwe Water Distribution System

[ Photo Spot: Miondwe Water Infrastructure ]

Project Overview

Building on the successful solar power system installed at the ranger camp in August 2025, the Miondwe Water project expands pipeline infrastructure and storage distribution systems to ensure clean water access.

Key Objectives

  • Extend distribution piping to local residential nodes.
  • Optimize pump capacity using existing solar infrastructure.
  • Conduct water quality testing and community maintenance training.
