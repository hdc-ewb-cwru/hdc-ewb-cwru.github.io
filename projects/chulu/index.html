Chulu Community Charging Station

[ Photo Spot: Chulu Charging Station Banner ]

Project Overview

The Chulu Community is located 2.5km from the park fence and currently lacks access to electricity. Our team is designing a community solar charging station to serve over 5,500 residents.

Key Objectives

  • Provide off-grid mobile phone and battery charging.
  • Implement a sustainable, local operational billing model.
  • Utilize solar photovoltaic systems built for long-term rural durability.
