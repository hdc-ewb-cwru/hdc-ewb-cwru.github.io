Doan Brook Watershed Restoration

[ Photo Spot: Doan Brook Site ]

Project Overview

The Doan Brook project is our chapter's flagship local initiative in Cleveland. Students collaborate with local environmental agencies to implement sustainable urban watershed management and habitat restoration along Doan Brook.

Key Focus Areas

  • Water sampling, runoff monitoring, and environmental impact assessments.
  • Community cleanup days and streambank stabilization.
  • Applying engineering principles to urban ecology right on campus.
